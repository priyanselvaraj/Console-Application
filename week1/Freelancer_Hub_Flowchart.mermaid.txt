flowchart TD
    A([Start]) --> B[Client posts a project]
    B --> C[Freelancers view available projects]
    C --> D[Freelancer applies / is matched to project]
    D --> E{Client selects freelancer?}
    E -- No --> C
    E -- Yes --> F[Freelancer assigned to project]
    F --> G[Project broken into tasks]
    G --> H[Freelancer works on tasks]
    H --> I{Task completed?}
    I -- No --> H
    I -- Yes --> J[Mark task as complete]
    J --> K{All tasks done?}
    K -- No --> H
    K -- Yes --> L[Client reviews completed project]
    L --> M[Payment processed]
    M --> N([End])
